Paddy organizes the information you provide. It does not choose medicines or prescribe doses.

01

Read. Review. Save.

Recognize packaging text on your phone. Review the name, strength, quantity and expiry before confirming. Manual entry is available too.

02

Know what is there.

Keep packages and their expiry dates separate. See usable stock, expired supplies and estimates of when scheduled medicines may run out.

03

Follow your own routine.

Enter the times and amounts from your medication instructions. Answer each scheduled dose and correct mistakes without losing track of the current stock.

The Android MVP stores medicine records locally and performs text recognition on the device. There is no developer-run medicine cloud, advertising or analytics service in this version.

Your operating system may separately back up app data according to its settings. Read the policy for temporary photos, support email and website hosting.

Does Paddy decide which medicine I should take?

No. You enter the medicine, dose and routine. Follow your prescription and medicine instructions, and consult a healthcare professional for medical decisions.

Can I rely only on an alarm?

Notifications depend on your phone, operating-system settings and permissions. A displayed or dismissed notification is not proof that a medicine was taken. Check the saved occurrence before acting on a repeated or delayed reminder.

Do packaging photos leave my phone?

The app does not upload photos to an OCR server. It uses temporary local copies for recognition and removes them when no longer needed. Original gallery photos remain yours.
