Who is responsible and how to contact us

The developer and website operator is Solutions Marcin Szymczyk (Solutions MS), Poland. Contact: marcin.szymczyk@solutionsms.pl. Company website: solutionsms.pl. This policy describes the current Android MVP; it does not describe future premium/cloud services.

Medicine data stays in the app

The app stores the information you enter or confirm: medicine names and strengths, package quantities and expiry/opening dates, your profile name if provided, intake rules, occurrence responses, intake history and settings. Recognition and these records are processed locally on your phone. The developer does not automatically receive your medicine database, packaging photos or intake history. The current app has no server account, developer-run synchronization, advertising or analytics service.

Camera and selected photos

Scanning starts when you choose a photo or use the camera. The app processes the image on the device to extract text. App-created temporary image copies are removed when no longer needed; leftovers from an interrupted scan are cleaned up during a later scan or startup. Original gallery images are not deleted. No photo collection for AI training is operated.

Storage, notifications and system backup

Records are stored in the app’s private, operating-system-protected storage. This is not a claim of additional application-level database encryption. Notifications use generic prompts to reduce disclosure of medicine details. Notification and exact-alarm access are controlled through Android permissions/settings.

The current Android app does not explicitly exclude its user data from system backup. Depending on the device, provider and settings, the operating system may back up or transfer app data, including the database, separately from the app’s own local processing. Backup protection, retention and deletion are governed by that service and your settings. The developer does not operate or receive this system backup. See Android backup information.

Keeping and deleting records

Medicine records remain locally until you remove them or clear the app’s data. You can edit/delete inventory entries in the app. To clear all local app data, use Android’s app storage settings; names vary by device. This removes saved inventory, history and settings. Uninstalling also removes local app data, but a system backup or later restore may be handled separately. Manage any such copies in the backup provider’s settings. Clearing app data does not delete your original gallery photos.

If you contact the developer

We use ordinary support correspondence to answer requests and protect legitimate interests in handling enquiries and claims (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, where applicable). Correspondence is retained for handling the matter and, where necessary, establishing, exercising or defending legal claims; the period depends on the matter and applicable obligations. Support messages are not a medicine synchronization service and are not used to train an AI model.

See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement. Following an external link or sending email is subject to the destination/provider’s policies.

Your rights and policy changes

For personal data processed by the developer, you can contact marcin.szymczyk@solutionsms.pl about access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and other applicable rights. Their availability depends on the processing and legal basis. You may complain to the Polish supervisory authority, UODO. We cannot remotely read, correct or delete the medicine database held only on your phone. A local profile is not an online account. Material changes to app data handling will be reflected in an updated dated policy; future cloud functionality will require its own updated disclosures before use.
